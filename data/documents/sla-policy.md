# Support SLA Policy

Applies to every service-desk ticket in all four regions. Hours are business hours in the customer's time zone, except P1, which is measured around the clock.

## Targets

| Priority | Meaning | First response | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Customer operations stopped (e.g. safety stock missing for a site) | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| P2 | Serious impact, workaround exists | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| P3 | Standard request or question | 8 hours | 72 hours |
| P4 | Low impact, information only | 24 hours | 120 hours |

## Breach

A ticket breaches its SLA when resolution takes longer than the target. The SLA breach rate is breached tickets divided by all tickets opened in the period. The company target is below 5% overall and below 10% for P1.

## Reporting

Support leads review the breach rate by region and priority every week. A region above 15% P1 breach rate for a week must follow the P1 Escalation Playbook and request cover (see Support Staffing and After-Hours Cover).
