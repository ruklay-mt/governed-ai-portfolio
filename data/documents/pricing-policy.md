# Pricing and Discount Policy

Owner: Commercial Finance. Applies to all regions (NZ-NORTH, NZ-SOUTH, AU-EAST, AU-WEST).

## List prices

List prices are set nationally in NZD and converted to AUD for Australian customers at the monthly rate published by Finance. Prices exclude GST (15% in New Zealand, 10% in Australia). Sales reps must quote from the current price list; hand-typed prices are not allowed in the ERP.

## Discount cap

The standard discount cap is **15% off list price per order line**, for every product category, including Safety Equipment. A discount above 15% is an exception and needs approval before the order is confirmed (see the Discount Approval Process).

Volume pricing agreed in a signed customer contract counts towards the cap. Promotional prices set by Head Office do not.

## Why the cap exists

Safety Equipment and Workwear run on thin margins (gross margin typically 45–50%). A 25% discount on a Safety Equipment line can remove more than half of its gross margin. Revenue growth bought with deep discounts is not counted towards sales targets.

## Monitoring

Finance reviews discount levels by rep, region and category every month. Lines above the cap without a recorded approval are reported to the regional sales manager. Repeated breaches lead to a review of the rep's discount authority.

## Rounding and freight

Prices are rounded to the nearest cent. Freight is charged at cost for orders under NZD 250 and free above that threshold, unless a contract says otherwise.
