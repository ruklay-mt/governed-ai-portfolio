# Warranty Policy

## Warranty periods

| Category | Warranty |
|---|---|
| Power Tools | 3 years (battery packs 2 years) |
| Camping, Hiking, Climbing | 2 years |
| Water Sports | 2 years (inflatables 1 year) |
| Lighting | 2 years |
| Safety Equipment, Workwear, Hand Tools | 1 year |

The warranty starts on the delivery date on the invoice.

## Repair, replace or refund

1. Ask for the order number and photos of the fault.
2. If the fault is a known issue with a published knowledge-base article, follow the article.
3. Otherwise, offer a replacement for items under NZD 200, and a repair assessment for items above.
4. Refund only when the item cannot be repaired or replaced within 15 business days.

## What is not covered

Normal wear, misuse, modification, and damage from use outside the product's rating (for example, a non-rated harness used for fall arrest). Climbing and fall-arrest equipment involved in a fall must be retired, not repaired.

## Supplier claims

Support logs every warranty claim with the product SKU and batch number when available. Product Management claims the cost back from the supplier when a batch has a confirmed defect.
