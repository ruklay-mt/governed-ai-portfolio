# Regional Sales Guide: AU-WEST

Territory: Perth, Fremantle, Bunbury, Kalgoorlie, Geraldton. Our smallest region.

## Customers

Mining and resources contractors in the Goldfields and Mid West, marine and water-sports businesses around Perth and Fremantle, and construction firms in the metro area.

## Seasonality

Mining customers buy Safety Equipment and Lighting all year. Water Sports peak in summer.

## Focus for 2026

- Mining contractors: annual supply agreements for Safety Equipment and Lighting.
- Rebuild trust with customers affected by slow support in January 2026; check in on open tickets during account visits.

## Practical notes

Prices are in AUD. Stock ships from Perth; Kalgoorlie and Geraldton deliveries take two to three days. The support team here is small, so tell support early about large deliveries that may need setup help.
