# Product Range Overview

Harbourline Supply Co. distributes about 1,500 products in 12 categories to trade and retail customers across New Zealand and Australia.

## Outdoor

- **Camping**: tents, sleeping bags, stoves, chairs, coolers, tarps. Best-seller: TrailPeak 2-Person Tent.
- **Hiking**: daypacks, trekking poles, rain jackets, gaiters, hydration packs.
- **Climbing**: harnesses, ropes, carabiners, helmets, belay devices.
- **Water Sports**: kayaks, paddles, life jackets, wetsuits, dry bags.

## Trade

- **Safety Equipment**: hard hats, hi-vis vests, safety glasses, ear muffs, fall-arrest kits. All items meet AS/NZS standards.
- **Workwear**: boots, pants, softshell jackets, gloves, thermal tops.
- **Power Tools** and **Hand Tools**: drills, saws, grinders, spanners, pliers, levels.
- **Lighting**: headlamps, work lights, lanterns, torches, flood lights.

## Seasonal

Outdoor categories peak in the Southern Hemisphere summer (December–February). Heating products peak in winter (June–August). Trade categories sell evenly through the year.

## Product codes

SKUs start with a category prefix, for example CMP (Camping), SAF (Safety Equipment), PWR (Power Tools), followed by a four-digit number.
