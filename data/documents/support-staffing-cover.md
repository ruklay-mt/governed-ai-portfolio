# Support Staffing and After-Hours Cover

## Normal staffing

Each region has its own support team: NZ-NORTH and AU-EAST have 6 agents each, NZ-SOUTH 4 and AU-WEST 3. Teams work 8am–6pm local time. A national on-call agent covers P1 tickets outside these hours.

## Planned leave

No more than one third of a team may be on leave at the same time. January is the busiest leave period; leads must plan summer leave by 1 November.

## Requesting cover

When a team is short (leave, illness, vacancies) and the backlog grows:

1. The regional lead asks for cover in the national support channel.
2. Agents from another region take that region's P3 and P4 tickets first, so local agents can focus on P1 and P2.
3. If the P1 breach rate is above 15% for a week, the Head of Support may approve overtime or temporary staff.

## Lessons from January 2026

AU-WEST was short-staffed through most of January 2026 while ticket volume rose. P1 and P2 tickets breached SLA together. Cover from AU-EAST was requested late. The rule since then: request cover as soon as two agents in a team of three or fewer are away.
