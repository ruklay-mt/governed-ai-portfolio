# Discount Approval Process

This process applies to any order line with a discount above the 15% cap in the Pricing and Discount Policy.

## Who can approve

| Discount on the line | Approver |
|---|---|
| up to 15% | the sales rep (no approval needed) |
| 15.01% – 20% | regional sales manager |
| 20.01% – 30% | national sales manager and Commercial Finance |
| above 30% | not allowed, except clearance stock set by Head Office |

## How to request approval

1. Create the quote in the ERP with the proposed discount. The quote is held automatically.
2. Add a reason: competitor match (attach the competitor quote), volume commitment, or strategic account.
3. The approver accepts or rejects within one business day. Accepted quotes are released for order entry.

## What reviewers check

- The margin left on the line after discount. Lines below 20% gross margin are rejected unless the account is strategic.
- Whether the same customer has had repeated exceptions in the last quarter.
- Whether the discount is concentrated in one category or one rep.

## Records

Approvals are stored against the quote number. An over-cap line with no approval record is treated as a policy breach in the monthly discount review.
