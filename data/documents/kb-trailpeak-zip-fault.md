# KB: TrailPeak 2-Person Tent zip fault

Product: TrailPeak 2-Person Tent (SKU CMP-0001). Status: confirmed supplier defect.

## Symptoms

The main door zip separates behind the slider after a few uses, or the slider jams at the top corner. Customers usually report it after their first one or two trips.

## Affected stock

Tents from zip batch **ZB-2602**, received at the Auckland warehouse in late February 2026 and sold mostly in NZ-NORTH from March to April 2026. The batch code is printed on the white label inside the tent bag.

## What to do

1. Confirm the batch code. If it is ZB-2602, treat the claim as a known fault: no photos needed.
2. Offer a replacement tent from a later batch, or a full refund if the customer prefers.
3. Book the return with reason **Faulty zip** so it is counted in the supplier claim.
4. Tag the ticket "Product fault" and link it to this article.

If the batch code is different, follow the normal Warranty Policy.

## Background

Returns of this tent in NZ-NORTH ran at about four times the normal rate in March–April 2026. The supplier has replaced the zip supplier and credited the batch.
