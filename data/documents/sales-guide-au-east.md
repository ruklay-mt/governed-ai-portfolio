# Regional Sales Guide: AU-EAST

Territory: Sydney, Brisbane, Newcastle, Gold Coast, Canberra. Our largest region by revenue.

## Customers

Construction groups in Sydney and Brisbane, mining services in the Hunter, government buyers in Canberra, and outdoor retailers on the coast. Several key accounts with large annual spend are in this region.

## Seasonality

Safety Equipment, Workwear and Power Tools sell evenly through the year. Outdoor categories peak in summer.

## Focus for 2026

- Hold margin on Safety Equipment. Competition from importers is strong; compete on availability and compliance support, not on discounts above the cap.
- Grow Power Tools with construction groups through trade-account pricing.

## Practical notes

Prices are in AUD, converted from the national NZD list. Stock ships from the Sydney warehouse; Brisbane is next-day.
