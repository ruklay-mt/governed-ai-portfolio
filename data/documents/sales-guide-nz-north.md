# Regional Sales Guide: NZ-NORTH

Territory: Auckland, Hamilton, Tauranga, Whangarei, Napier. Our largest New Zealand region by customer count.

## Customers

A mix of outdoor retailers in Auckland and Tauranga, and construction and horticulture businesses in the Waikato and Bay of Plenty. Local councils buy Safety Equipment and Workwear on annual contracts.

## Seasonality

Camping and Water Sports peak from November to February. Pre-season orders from retailers arrive in September and October; push early-order deals then.

## Focus for 2026

- Grow Workwear with horticulture customers.
- Keep retailers confident in Camping after the TrailPeak tent returns in March–April 2026: replacement stock is from a new batch.

## Practical notes

Freight from the Auckland warehouse is next-day for most of the territory. Napier orders ship from Auckland with two-day delivery.
