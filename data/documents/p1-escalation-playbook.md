# P1 Escalation Playbook

## When a ticket is P1

The customer cannot operate: a site is missing safety equipment it needs to work, a fleet of tools has failed, or an order for a time-critical job is lost. When in doubt, raise as P1 and downgrade later.

## First hour

1. Acknowledge the customer within 1 hour by phone, not only email.
2. Assign an owner. The owner stays on the ticket until it is resolved or handed over in person.
3. Post the ticket in the regional P1 channel.

## Hours 2 to 6

- Check stock in other warehouses; inter-region transfers are approved for P1 without a manager.
- Update the customer every 2 hours.
- If no fix is in sight by hour 4, escalate to the regional support lead.

## At risk of breach

If a P1 will not be resolved within 8 hours, the support lead informs the regional sales manager and the customer's account owner, and agrees a recovery plan with the customer.

## After resolution

Record the root cause category. Clusters of P1 breaches in one region are reviewed in the weekly SLA meeting.
