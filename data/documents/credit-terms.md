# Credit Terms Policy

Owner: Credit Control, Finance.

## Standard terms

All trade accounts are on **30-day terms from invoice date** unless a signed contract says otherwise. Invoices are issued in the customer's currency (NZD or AUD).

## Credit limits

New accounts start with a credit limit of NZD 10,000 (or AUD equivalent). Limits above NZD 50,000 need approval from the Finance Manager and a credit check renewed every year.

## Days overdue and ageing buckets

Days overdue = days since the due date for any invoice not fully paid. Accounts receivable is reported in buckets: current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days overdue.

## When an account is placed on hold

- Any invoice 60+ days overdue: new orders need Finance approval.
- Any invoice 90+ days overdue, or the balance is above the credit limit: account on stop.

## Key accounts

Large accounts that pay later each quarter are a credit risk even when they always pay in the end. Finance reviews the trend in average days to pay for the top 20 accounts every quarter and raises a review when it rises for two quarters in a row.

## Confidentiality

Payment history, credit limits and overdue balances are Finance information. They are not shared with sales or support staff.
