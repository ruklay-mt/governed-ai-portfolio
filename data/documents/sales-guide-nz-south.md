# Regional Sales Guide: NZ-SOUTH

Territory: Christchurch, Dunedin, Queenstown, Nelson, Invercargill.

## Customers

Outdoor and adventure tourism operators in Queenstown, farming and rural suppliers in Canterbury and Southland, and construction firms in Christchurch.

## Seasonality

Two peaks: summer for Camping, Hiking and Climbing (December–February), and winter for Heating and Workwear (June–August), when southern sites need thermal gear.

## Focus for 2026

- Tourism operators: bundle Climbing and Safety Equipment with fitting and inspection advice.
- Rural suppliers: grow Workwear and Hand Tools through stocking agreements.

## Practical notes

Stock ships from the Christchurch warehouse. Queenstown and Invercargill deliveries take two days; plan winter orders early, as roads over the passes can close.

## Discounts

Stay within the 15% cap. Tourism operators often ask for deeper discounts before summer; use the Discount Approval Process and offer volume terms instead.
