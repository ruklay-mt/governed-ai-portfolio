# Collections Procedure

Follow these steps for every overdue invoice. Record each contact in the account notes.

## Timeline

| Days overdue | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Automatic reminder email with a copy of the invoice |
| 14 | Phone call from Credit Control; confirm the invoice is not disputed |
| 30 | Formal letter; new orders need Finance approval |
| 60 | Account on hold; regional sales manager informed (no payment details shared) |
| 90 | Account on stop; consider a payment plan or external collection |

## Disputed invoices

If the customer disputes an amount, raise a support ticket in the "Billing" category and pause reminders on that invoice only. Resolve disputes within 10 business days.

## Payment plans

Credit Control may agree a payment plan of up to 3 months for balances under NZD 100,000. Larger plans need the Finance Manager. Interest is not charged on agreed plans.

## Key accounts

For a key account that is paying later each quarter, schedule a review meeting with the customer's accounts payable team before the account reaches 60 days overdue, and agree a target for days to pay.
