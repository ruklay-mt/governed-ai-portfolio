# Returns Policy

Applies to all customers in New Zealand and Australia. Nothing in this policy limits rights under the NZ Consumer Guarantees Act or Australian Consumer Law.

## Change-of-mind returns

Unused products in original packaging can be returned within **30 days** of delivery for a credit note. A 10% restocking fee applies to change-of-mind returns over NZD 500. Custom-cut rope, opened safety consumables and clearance items cannot be returned for change of mind.

## Faulty or damaged products

Products that are faulty, damaged in transit or not as described can be returned at any time within the warranty period. There is no restocking fee and Harbourline pays return freight. See the Warranty Policy for repair-or-replace rules.

## How returns are recorded

1. The customer or rep raises a support ticket in the "Returns" category.
2. Support issues a return authorisation (RA) number.
3. When the goods arrive, the warehouse books a return line in the ERP: a negative-quantity line on a new order with a **return reason** (Changed mind, Wrong item ordered, Damaged in transit, Not as described, Faulty zip, and so on).

## Return rate monitoring

The return rate is returned units divided by units sold for the same product and period. The normal rate is 2–4% for most categories. A product above 3× its usual rate for two weeks triggers a quality review with the supplier, and support may publish a knowledge-base article for the fault.
